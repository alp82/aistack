The Mechanics

fit · rotation · draft slots

This view is not part of the page. It shows the three rules this round proposes, so each one can be judged on its own. Everything here runs on the machine at sync time, deterministically.

1 · What fit means

The score that ranks the pool and picks the three surfaced slots

fit = coverage × surprise

  • coverage: the share of synced harnesses the metric counts. A metric from local Git history counts them all.
  • surprise: how far the value sits from the typical band its metric rule declares, normalized 0 to 1. The band is part of the versioned rule, like a proxy match.
  • Ties break on movement: the change against the prior window.
  • A fact another block or section already shows is excluded from the slots. Today that excludes the subagent share (Actual Usage) and the git shape (the Git Ledger).
  • No LLM ranks anything. Fit is computed, logged, and reproducible (ADR-0001).

3 · When the slots rotate

Recompute always, swap rarely

  • Fit recomputes at every sync.
  • A challenger takes a slot only when its fit beats the incumbent by 25% or more.
  • An incumbent leaves at once when its coverage drops (a harness stopped syncing) or its signal had no events in the window.
  • At most one slot swaps per sync day, so the section never turns over wholesale.

4 · The draft slots

Where the LLM writes, per ADR-0001

  • Slot 1 · the section lead: how this person works, in a few sentences over the whole aggregate set.
  • Slot 2 · the anatomy note: what the phase split says about the flow.
  • Slot 3 · the routing note: what the model split says about the policy.
  • Input: approved aggregates only. The draft never states a number, a label, or a legend. The measured figures stand beside it.
  • Each draft is labeled, the owner edits it, and an unapproved draft renders nothing. A missing measurement stays absent.
  • Everything else on the section is measured text: box captions and tags belong to the metric rules, not to the LLM.
