Nvidia agreed to a $6 billion non-exclusive license for Poolside's model factory and plans to hire 109 employees. Nvidia will also invest $1 billion in Poolside at a $12 billion pre-money valuation. Poolside's founders remain while the company develops a new direction after it couldn't finance a planned 40,000-GPU cluster. A separate infrastructure company is building a 1.2-gigawatt Texas data center, moving part of the remaining business toward AI compute.
