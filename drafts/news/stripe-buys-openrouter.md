Latent Space reports that Stripe is close to acquiring OpenRouter for roughly $7 billion, though neither company has formally confirmed the deal. OpenRouter serves about eight million developers and routes 250 trillion tokens each month, five times its February volume. The company reports $140 million in annualized revenue and $100 million in annualized gross profit. The deal would give Stripe a major position in the model-routing layer used by application developers.
