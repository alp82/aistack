headline: [AINews] Memory prices up 500% in 12 months
topic: AI industry
---

DDR5 memory prices have surged, with high-capacity kits approaching a 500% year-over-year increase. A 128 GB DDR5 kit now lists for $3,399, about ten times its lowest tracked price. Latent Space connects the shortage to AI infrastructure demand, while Tom's Hardware says hyperscalers reserved almost all 2027 global DRAM capacity. PC builders and device makers now compete for the supply left after AI data centers secure priority.
