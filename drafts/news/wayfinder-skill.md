topic: Agent engineering
---

Matt Pocock released `/wayfinder` to manage projects whose final shape can't be decided at the start. The skill helps developers and other planners coordinate agent sessions for research, prototyping, interviews, and human tasks. It keeps settled decisions in a central map while giving each session a focused ticket. Pocock recommends `grill-me` for clear, single-session plans and `/wayfinder` when the work requires progressive discovery.
