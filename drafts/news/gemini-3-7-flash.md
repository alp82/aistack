Google introduced Gemini 3.7 Flash as a faster, more capable update to its Flash model line. The release targets developers using the Gemini API, Google AI Studio, Antigravity, and other supported surfaces. Google offers promotional pricing at 50% below Gemini 3.6 Flash through December 2026. Latent Space places the release in Google DeepMind's effort to regain ground after recent Flash models trailed newer competitors.
